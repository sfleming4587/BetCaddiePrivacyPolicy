BetCaddie Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how BetCaddie ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the BetCaddie mobile application (the "App"). If you have any questions, contact us at sfleming4587@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

  • Anonymous account identifier. When you use the App, we use Firebase Anonymous Authentication, which assigns your device a random, anonymous identifier. We do not collect your real name, email address, or a password to create an account.
  • Tournament information you enter. If you create or join a tournament, the information you provide — a display name you choose, golf scores, bets and settlements, and tournament join codes — is stored in the cloud so it can sync among the participants of that tournament.
  • On-device data. Local (non-tournament) rounds, app settings, and archived tournaments are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to us.

We do not collect your real name (unless you choose to enter it as a display name), email address, payment or financial information, precise location, contacts, or advertising identifiers. BetCaddie tracks friendly bets but does not process, hold, or transfer any money.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information solely to operate the App — to create and run tournaments, sync scores in real time among participants, and calculate and settle bets. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not use analytics or tracking technologies.

3. How Information Is Stored and Shared

  • Cloud data is stored using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Google processes this data on our behalf as a service provider. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Tournament information is visible to the other people participating in a tournament you create or join.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Cloud tournament data is removed from our servers after a tournament is finished and archived, and may also be removed after a period of inactivity. Data stored on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the App. To request deletion of data we hold, contact us at sfleming4587@gmail.com.

5. How We Keep Information Safe

We rely on reasonable technical measures, including Google Firebase's security infrastructure, to protect information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

BetCaddie is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us at sfleming4587@gmail.com and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under California's CCPA and other US state privacy laws, or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, or to object to its processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at sfleming4587@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and post the new version on this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, email us at sfleming4587@gmail.com.
