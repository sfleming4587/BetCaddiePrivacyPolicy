1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Personal information you disclose to us

In Short: We collect information that you provide to us, plus an anonymous identifier needed to operate the app.

We collect the following information:

  • Anonymous account identifier. When you use the App, we use Firebase Anonymous Authentication, which assigns your device a random, anonymous identifier. We do not collect your real name, email address, or a password to create an account.
  • Tournament information you enter. If you create or join a tournament, we store the information you provide — a display name you choose, golf scores, bets and settlements, and tournament join codes — in the cloud so it can sync among the participants of that tournament.
  • On-device data. Local (non-tournament) rounds, app settings, and archived tournaments are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to us.
  • Technical data. When the App connects to our cloud services, Google Firebase may automatically receive limited technical data, such as your IP address, as a normal part of providing authentication and database services.

We do not collect your real name (unless you choose to use it as a display name), email address, payment or financial information, precise location, contacts, or advertising identifiers. BetCaddie tracks friendly bets but does not process, hold, or transfer any money.

Sensitive Information

We do not process sensitive information.

2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

In Short: We process your information to provide and operate the App.

We process your personal information for the following reasons:

  • To create and run tournaments and groups;

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not use analytics or tracking technologies.

3. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION?

In Short: We share information with our service provider (Google Firebase) and with the other participants of tournaments you join.

We may share your information in the following situations:

  • Service Providers. We use Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to store and synchronize cloud data. Google processes this data on our behalf as a service provider. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Other Tournament Participants. Information you enter in a tournament (such as your display name, scores, and bets) is visible to the other people participating in that tournament.
  • Business Transfers. We may share or transfer your information in connection with, or during negotiations of, any merger, sale of assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

4. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

In Short: We keep cloud data only as long as a tournament is active; on-device data stays until you remove it.

Cloud tournament data is removed from our servers after a tournament is finished and archived, and may also be removed after a period of inactivity. Information stored on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the App. When we no longer need to process your information, we will delete it or anonymize it.
